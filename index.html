About the colour tags. Food tag (solid): a simplified general guide, not an official nutrition score or medical advice — green “choose more often”, orange “consider portion & frequency”, red “occasional choice”. Low calories doesn't automatically mean healthy, and no food is forbidden. Diary row colour = portion: compares your amount with a reference serving chosen by this app (label serve or usual amount) — up to 1× = within reference, over 1× to 2× = larger, over 2× = much larger. Reference servings are app conventions, not safe limits or recommended intakes. Your daily total is what matters.
